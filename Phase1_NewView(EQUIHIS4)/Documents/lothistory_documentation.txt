LOTHISTORY Business description and related fields:

Executive Summary

Field / Concept: LOT
Business meaning: The unit of work processed through the fab. A lot contains one or more wafers and is the primary WIP and genealogy object.

Field / Concept: OPER
Business meaning: A specific process step on a route. It answers: where is the lot in the manufacturing flow?

Field / Concept: COMMAND
Business meaning: The transaction or system action performed against the lot. It answers: what action was taken?

Field / Concept: HISTCODE
Business meaning: The specific history event or attribute recorded by a command, such as MOVE IN, MOVE OUT, ROUTE, COMMENT, or LOT COMPLETE.

Field / Concept: HISTORDER
Business meaning: A field used in lot-history extracts to distinguish or order records that share the same transaction context. The exact database contract should be validated against the authoritative schema.

Field / Concept: MASK_LVL
Business meaning: An operation property associated with the process step. Internal documentation confirms operations have a mask level. It is the masking level that operations are associated with. It can also be thought of as layer.

Field / Concept: OPERATION_DESC
Business meaning: The human-readable operation description. OPER should be treated as the identifier; descriptions should be interpreted with route and historical context.


1. LOT Lifecycle

What a LOT represents

In NetVision, a LOT is the unit of work processed through the fab. It contains one or more wafers, typically 25, and carries the information needed to manage current WIP position, route, operation, machine assignment, dispatch area, masks, priority, sales-order linkage, attributes, and manufacturing history.

End-to-end business lifecycle

Stage: 1. Define product flow
Business activity: A part is assigned to a route. The route contains an ordered sequence of runcards and operations.
Typical records or outcomes: Approved route and operation sequence.

Stage: 2. Create the lot
Business activity: LotCreate establishes the lot with part, route, owner, wafer, mask, history, and initial WIP details. For applicable production lots, raw wafer inventory is decremented in Great Plains.
Typical records or outcomes: LTCR command; CR, PT, RT, WF and related history entries.

Stage: 3. Initial dispatch
Business activity: After creation, eligible non-test-wafer lots are automatically dispatched to the first operation when required route-operation-machine and recipe information is available.
Typical records or outcomes: Initial WIP and dispatch state.

Stage: 4. Process through route
Business activity: The lot moves through operations using move-in and move-out activity. NetVision validates resources and captures genealogy, EDC, SPC, comments, status, and location history.
Typical records or outcomes: MVIN/MOUT and movement-related history.

Stage: 5. Handle exceptions
Business activity: The lot may be held, released, reworked, split, merged, repositioned, rerouted, or adjusted under business rules.
Typical records or outcomes: HOLD, REWK, SPLT, LMRG, LRTE, LOPR and related history.

Stage: 6. Stage for transfer
Business activity: The completed fabrication lot reaches the TRANSFER operation or staging area and must satisfy transfer validation, including applicable inspection and quality requirements.
Typical records or outcomes: Transfer eligibility.

Stage: 7. Complete and transfer
Business activity: LotTransfer marks the lot completed, records completion data, removes it from active WIP, and may create a finished-goods inventory transaction in Great Plains.
Typical records or outcomes: TRNF command; LC history; closed-to-manufacturing state.

Stage: 8. Reporting and downstream use
Business activity: Completed lot information remains available for reporting, labels, quality, traceability, and genealogy.
Typical records or outcomes: Reporting record and retained history.


2. Operations and Commands

OPER: the process step
An operation is a specific process step that a lot goes through. At minimum, an operation has an operation number, description, mask level, and dispatch area. Operations are ordered within runcards and routes. A lot is placed at the first route operation when created and progresses through the ordered flow.

COMMAND: the transaction
A command identifies the transaction or applications action that caused the lot-history records to be written. One command can generate multiple HISTCODE rows because a single business transaction may record several effects or attributes.

Command: LTCR
Plain-language name: Lot Create
Role in lifecycle: Creates a fabrication lot and its initial manufacturing records.

Command: MVIN
Plain-language name: Move In
Role in lifecycle: Records entry into processing at the current operation or machine.

Command: MOUT
Plain-language name: Move Out
Role in lifecycle: Records completion or exit from the current processing step and may advance the lot.

Command: DISP
Plain-language name: Dispatch
Role in lifecycle: Assigns or requests a lot for processing.

Command: HOLD
Plain-language name: Hold
Role in lifecycle: Changes or records lot hold status and comments.

Command: SPLT
Plain-language name: Lot Split
Role in lifecycle: Creates a child lot or separates wafers from the original lot.

Command: LMRG
Plain-language name: Lot Merge
Role in lifecycle: Combines eligible lots or quantities.

Command: REWK
Plain-language name: Rework
Role in lifecycle: Routes a full or partial lot through an approved rework path.

Command: LOPR
Plain-language name: Change Lot Operation
Role in lifecycle: Adjusts the lot to another operation under business rules.

Command: LRTE
Plain-language name: Change Lot Route
Role in lifecycle: Changes the route and records associated operation/route effects.

Command: RPOS
Plain-language name: Reposition
Role in lifecycle: Repositions or requests the lot within an operation.

Command: SETP/SETE
Plain-language name: Set Production / Engineering Flags
Role in lifecycle: Updates production- or engineering-controlled lot attributes.

Command: RPUR
Plain-language name: Repurpose Lot
Role in lifecycle: Recreates scrap material for approved engineering use.

Command: TRNF
Plain-language name: Transfer
Role in lifecycle: Completes and closes the lot and performs the final manufacturing-to-inventory handoff when configured.

Interpretation rule: Use OPER to understand process position, COMMAND to understand the initiating transaction, and HISTCODE to understand each detailed effect recorded by that transaction.


3. History Codes and Order

HISTCODE
HISTCODE identifies the specific event, attribute, or state change written to lot history. The command-to-history mapping shows that the same HISTCODE can appear under different commands and that one command commonly produces several history rows.

Code: CR
History label: CREATE LOT
Business description: Creation of the lot.

Code: IN
History label: MOVE IN
Business description: Lot entered processing.

Code: MV
History label: MOVE OUT
Business description: Lot exited or completed a processing step.

Code: TX
History label: TRANSITION
Business description: Lot is transitioning into move-in.

Code: LA
History label: ARRIVAL
Business description: Lot arrived at an operation.

Code: DL
History label: DISPATCH
Business description: Lot was dispatched.

Code: SK/SA
History label: SKIP OPER / Auto Skip
Business description: Operation was skipped manually or automatically.

Code: RT
History label: ROUTE
Business description: Route associated with or changed for the lot.

Code: OP
History label: ADJ OPER
Business description: Operation adjustment.

Code: SP
History label: SPLIT LOT
Business description: Lot split transaction.

Code: MM/MQ
History label: MERGE LOTS / MERGE QTY
Business description: Lot merge and resulting quantity.

Code: RW
History label: REWORK
Business description: Lot rework.

Code: R1/R2
History label: RTE LEAVE/RTE RETURN
Business description: Departure from and return to the main route.

Code: HC
History label: HOLD CODE
Business description: Lot hold status or reason.

Code: LS
History label: CHG STATUS
Business description: Lot status change.

Code: CM/CA
History label: COMMENT/ Automation Comment
Business description: User or automated explanatory comment.

Code: PT
History label: PART NUM
Business description: Lot part number.

Code: MR
History label: MASK/REV
Business description: Mask number or revision associated with the lot.

Code: OW
History label: OWNERCODE
Business description: Lot owner code.

Code: DD
History label: DUE DATE
Business description: Lot due date.

Code: HT
History label: HOT STATUS
Business description: Hot-lot flag or priority status.

Code: SO
History label: SALES ORDR
Business description: Sales-order association.

Code: QI/QD/QE
History label: Quality Incident / Description / Event
Business description: Quality event and supporting description.

Code: SC
History label: ADHOCSCRAP
Business description: Ad hoc wafer scrap.

Code: UN
History label: UNSCRAP
Business description: Previously scrapped wafers were restored.

Code: LC
History label: LOTCOMPLET
Business description: Lot completed during transfer.

HISTORDER
Multiple lines were needed for the history entry. This is the order that they were entered.


4. Other Key Fields

MASK_LVL
This is the Mask level or the layer. Masks are used to define objects/structures. Different layers build different parts of the components.

OPER and OPERATION_DESC
OPER is the operation identifier or number of a step on a route. OPERATION_DESC is the display description is the human readble text associated with an operation in a route. Operation number and description are separate operation properties.


5. How to Read a Lot-History Row

Field: LOT
Example: 6053A0K9
Interpretation: The manufacturing lot whose history is being described.

Field: OPER
Example: 50325
Interpretation: The process operation associated with the event.

Field: OPERATION DESC
Example: LOW_TEMP
Interpretation: The human-readable operation description captured in the extract.

Field: COMMAND
Example: MOUT
Interpretation: The transaction was a Move-Out.

Field: HISTCODE
Example: MV
Interpretation: The detailed history event was MOVE OUT.

Field: HISTORDER
Example: 1
Interpretation: Order/differentiation within the transaction context; exact semantics require schema confirmation.

Field: MASK LVL
Example: 026
Interpretation: Operation mask/process-layer value; exact encoding requires data-dictionary confirmation.

Field: COMMENTS
Example: QTY: 25
Interpretation: Additional business detail recorded with the event.
