OPER

- Human-readable operation description.

COMMAND
- Business transaction that generated the history record.

HISTCODE
- Specific event or attribute recorded by the transaction.

HISTORDER
- Sequence of history records within the same transaction context.

MASK_LVL
- Process layer or mask level associated with the operation.

MACHINE
- Equipment or tool associated with the activity.

USERNAME / EMPID
- Operator or employee associated with the transaction.

DATE_TIME
- Timestamp of the manufacturing event.

TRANS
- Normalized event type used to classify activity.

HIST_REC
- A code that identifies the type of historical transaction being logged or comments. Use it to understand the origin and purpose of the recorded event within the lot's processing history.

LOT LifecycleWhat a LOT representsIn NetVision, a LOT is the unit of work processed through the fab. It contains one or more wafers, typically 25, and carries the information needed to manage current WIP position, route, operation, machine assignment, dispatch area, masks, priority, sales-order linkage, attributes, and manufacturing history.End-to-end business lifecycleStage: 1. Define product flowBusiness activity: A part is assigned to a route. The route contains an ordered sequence of runcards and operations.Typical records or outcomes: Approved route and operation sequence.Stage: 2. Create the lotBusiness activity: LotCreate establishes the lot with part, route, owner, wafer, mask, history, and initial WIP details. For applicable production lots, raw wafer inventory is decremented in Great Plains.Typical records or outcomes: LTCR command; CR, PT, RT, WF and related history entries.Stage: 3. Initial dispatchBusiness activity: After creation, eligible non-test-wafer lots are automatically dispatched to the first operation when required route-operation-machine and recipe information is available.Typical records or outcomes: Initial WIP and dispatch state.Stage: 4. Process through routeBusiness activity: The lot moves through operations using move-in and move-out activity. NetVision validates resources and captures genealogy, EDC, SPC, HIST_REC column handles comments or logs, status, and location history.Typical records or outcomes: MVIN/MOUT and movement-related history.Stage: 5. Handle exceptionsBusiness activity: The lot may be held, released, reworked, split, merged, repositioned, rerouted, or adjusted under business rules.Typical records or outcomes: HOLD, REWK, SPLT, LMRG, LRTE, LOPR and related history.Stage: 6. Stage for transferBusiness activity: The completed fabrication lot reaches the TRANSFER operation or staging area and must satisfy transfer validation, including applicable inspection and quality requirements.Typical records or outcomes: Transfer eligibility.Stage: 7. Complete and transferBusiness activity: LotTransfer marks the lot completed, records completion data, removes it from active WIP, and may create a finished-goods inventory transaction in Great Plains.Typical records or outcomes: TRNF command; LC history; closed-to-manufacturing state.Stage: 8. Reporting and downstream useBusiness activity: Completed lot information remains available for reporting, labels, quality, traceability, and genealogy.Typical records or outcomes: Reporting record and retained history.2.
